How to play

Light up the whole street with lanterns.

  1. Hang lanterns on the street tiles. A lantern lights its whole row and column, until a house blocks the light.
  2. Every street tile must be lit.
  3. Lanterns must not see each other. Two lanterns in the same row or column need a house between them.
  4. A number shows how many lanterns touch that house, directly above, below, left and right. Houses without a number can have any amount.

Every street has exactly one answer, and you can always find it by logic alone.
